# Disability Benefits
**Short-term disability (STD):** pays 70% of weekly earnings up to $1,200 per week. Benefits begin after a 7-calendar-day waiting period and last up to 17 weeks.
**Long-term disability (LTD):** starts after 119 days of disability (the end of STD). Pays 66 2/3% of monthly earnings up to $8,000 per month. For the first 24 months you must be unable to perform your own occupation. After that, you must be unable to perform any occupation you are suited for.
**Claims:** your physician completes the medical statement. Submit it within 30 days of your last day worked.
**Appeals:** a denied disability claim can be appealed in writing within 90 days of the decision. Appeals are handled by a human claims specialist.
