# Dental Coverage
**Basic services** (exams, cleanings, fillings, extractions): reimbursed at 80% on Standard and 100% on Enhanced.
**Major services** (crowns, bridges, dentures): reimbursed at 50% on both plans.
**Annual dental maximum:** $1,500 per person on Standard and $2,500 per person on Enhanced.
**Recall exams:** once every 9 months for adults and once every 6 months for children.
**Orthodontics:** reimbursed at 50% for dependent children under age 19, with a lifetime maximum of $2,000 per child.
**Pre-determination:** recommended before any treatment expected to cost more than $500. Submit the dentist's treatment plan through the portal.
