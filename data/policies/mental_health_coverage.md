# Mental Health Coverage
**Psychologists, registered social workers and psychotherapists:** reimbursed at 80%, up to $1,500 per calendar year on Standard and $2,500 on Enhanced. No physician referral is needed.
**Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** up to 6 free counselling sessions per issue, available 24/7 at 1-800-555-0177.
**In a crisis:** anyone in Canada can call or text 9-8-8 for the Suicide Crisis Helpline at any time.
