# Privacy and Identity Verification
Before discussing any plan details, verify the member with their full name, date of birth and policy number.
Staff and the virtual assistant never ask for a full card number, passwords, or a Social Insurance Number.
Members should not share these details in chat. If shared, the message is blocked or redacted.
Plan information is not shared with a spouse or other third party unless the member has given written authorization.
Calls and chats are recorded for quality and training. Member data is stored in Canada.
Members can request access to their personal information. We respond within 30 days.
