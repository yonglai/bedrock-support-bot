# Submitting a Claim
Claims must be submitted within 12 months of the date of the expense. Claims submitted later are not eligible.
**How to submit:** through the member portal or the mobile app. Upload a photo of the itemized receipt. Paper claims are also accepted by mail.
**Processing time:** online claims are processed within 5 business days. Paper claims take up to 10 business days.
**Payment:** reimbursement is paid by direct deposit. Set up direct deposit in your portal profile.
**Large claims:** claims over $5,000 may require additional supporting documents and can take longer.
**Direct billing:** many dentists, pharmacies and physiotherapists can bill Evergreen directly so you only pay your share.
