# Travel Emergency Medical Coverage
**Coverage:** emergency medical expenses up to $5,000,000 per person per trip, for trips of up to 60 days.
**Assistance line:** call 1-800-555-0142 within 24 hours of any emergency hospital admission. The assistance team arranges direct payment and can coordinate care.
**Pre-existing conditions:** covered only if medically stable for 90 days before departure.
**Exclusions:** travel to a destination where the Government of Canada advises "avoid all travel." Trip cancellation and interruption are not covered under this plan.
