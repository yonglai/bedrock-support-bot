# Evergreen Life Group Benefits: Health Plan Overview
Evergreen Life offers two group health plans: Standard and Enhanced.
**Standard plan:** annual deductible of $100 per person ($200 per family). After the deductible, eligible expenses are reimbursed at 80%.
**Enhanced plan:** no deductible. Eligible expenses are reimbursed at 90%.
**Paramedical services** (physiotherapy, chiropractic, massage therapy): up to $500 per practitioner type per calendar year on Standard and $1,000 on Enhanced.
**Waiting period:** new employees become eligible after a 3-month waiting period. Dependants are covered from the same date.
