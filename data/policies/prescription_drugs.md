# Prescription Drug Coverage
Prescription drugs are reimbursed at 80% on Standard and 90% on Enhanced.
**Generic substitution:** where a generic equivalent exists, reimbursement is based on the generic price.
**Prior authorization:** required for any drug that costs more than $1,500 per 30-day supply. Your physician submits the request, and a decision is made within 5 business days.
**Dispensing fee:** reimbursed up to $9.99 per prescription fill.
**Specialty drugs:** must be filled through the specialty pharmacy program.
**Vaccines:** publicly recommended adult vaccines are covered when prescribed by a pharmacist or physician.
