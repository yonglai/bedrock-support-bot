# Basic Group Life Insurance
**Coverage amount:** 2 times your annual earnings, rounded up to the next $1,000, to a maximum of $500,000.
**Evidence of insurability:** coverage above $300,000 requires a health questionnaire to be approved before it takes effect.
**Accidental death and dismemberment (AD&D):** equal to your basic life amount.
**Beneficiary changes:** update your beneficiary in the member portal or by submitting a signed paper form at any time.
**Conversion:** if your employment ends, you can convert your group coverage to an individual policy within 31 days without a medical exam.
