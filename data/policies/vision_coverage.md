# Vision Coverage
**Eyeglasses or contact lenses:** up to $250 every 24 months for adults. For children under 18, up to $150 every 12 months.
**Eye exams:** up to $100 every 24 months for adults.
**Laser eye surgery:** up to $500 per eye as a lifetime maximum. Available on the Enhanced plan only.
A valid prescription and an itemized receipt are required for all vision claims.
